# Scenario Definitions

## Scenario 1: PV + Battery
- Fully renewable system relying exclusively on photovoltaic generation and battery storage.
- No backup diesel generator.
- Results: LCOE = 0.1347 USD/kWh, Unmet Load = 0.77% (unacceptable for critical loads).

## Scenario 2: PV + Diesel Generator + Battery
- Integrates a diesel generator to improve supply reliability during periods of insufficient solar generation.
- Results: NPC = 1.23 M USD, LCOE = 0.1389 USD/kWh, Unmet Load = 0%.

## Scenario 3: PV + Wind + Diesel Generator + Battery (Optimal)
- Integrates wind generation with the PV-diesel-battery system to enhance renewable energy utilization and reduce diesel consumption.
- Optimal configuration: 391 kW PV, 20 kW wind, 2,134 kWh battery, 100 kW diesel generator.
- Results: NPC = 1.26 M USD, LCOE = 0.142 USD/kWh, RF = 97.1%, Unmet Load = 0%.

## Baseline: Diesel-Only System
- Two 100 kW diesel generator units sized to satisfy the same prioritized load demand.
- Results: NPC = 6.487 M USD, LCOE = 0.732 USD/kWh, CO₂ = 537,631 kg/year.
